Bernoulli's law in the LT cells

An ideal fluid in a pipe, written with two quantities only: radius R and time T. Squeeze the pipe: in the narrow part the flow speeds up and the pressure drops, while flow and head stay constant.

Throat radius0.62R₀
L³T⁻¹ · CONTINUITYQ = πR²·v = constThe same volume passes every section each second, so a narrower section must be faster: v ∝ 1/R².
T¹ · THE LT CLOCKT = 2πR / v ∝ R³Each section has its own period: the time the flow needs to travel one circumference 2πR. Halve the radius and the clock turns 8 times faster, the 3rd power of R.
L²T⁻² · HEAD½v² + p/ρ = constKinetic term and pressure term live in one cell and add up to the same head everywhere. Where the flow is fast, the pressure is low.

Ideal incompressible fluid, steady flow, no viscosity. Units: R₀ = 1, v₀ = 1 at the inlet, head E = 8.5 v₀². Density has dimension T⁻² in LT-System v2, so p/ρ has L²T⁻², the same cell as v² and the orbital M/r. This is the classical energy balance written in LT cells, not a new law.
