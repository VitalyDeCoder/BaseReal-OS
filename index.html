What We Are Building

BaseReal is the ultimate platform for launching unique universes!

Imagine an operating system — for an entire reality. The platform is fully decentralized: each launch creates an independent, autonomous world. All it takes is setting the mono-cursor of resources R and pressing Enter.

The position of this slider (from 0.1 to 0.9) sets the fundamental, unique character of the world:

  • Total Unity (Low R): A space where all egoistic boundaries are erased, and thoughts and resources are shared in a single collective mind. Users will gain a unique experience of complete dissolution in the shared flow and unrepeatable inner exultation!
  • Absolute Autonomy (High R): A world of pure individualism, where every inhabitant relies only on themselves, forging a steel will and independence under conditions of sharp scarcity.

You choose the rules of the game, and the Governing Neural Network ensures a flawless decentralized simulation. Where this choice leads and how the superposition unfolds is the main intrigue of your personal adventure!
