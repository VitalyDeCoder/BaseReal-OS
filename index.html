Seed of Reality

A universe built from two numbers.

BaseReal-OS is the operating system of a shared virtual universe. Every body in it — a star, a planet, a moon — is set by its radius and its period. Everything else follows from them.

Real Matter

Until someone looks inside, a planet is a shell: what it is made of has not been decided. Miners are the ones who look. They dig — and what they find becomes real matter, fixed once and for everyone.

They need it for a reason. Cities and spaceships cannot be built from numbers alone — they are built from matter that someone has actually mined.

The Universe and its State Registry

A complete virtual universe with one shared record: what exists, who made it, what has been measured. What is written stays written.

What nobody is looking at is neither computed nor stored — so the universe can be as large as the people in it need.

02

Foundation

Why it holds together: the whole engine stands on a few lines.

μ = 4π²R³ / T²

A body is two numbers

Mass is not a separate ingredient. It follows from the radius R and the period T of an orbit at the very surface.

P = T · (a / R)3/2

The 3/2 law

One rule gives every orbit its period: the closer to the star, the shorter the year. Moons, planets and double stars obey the same line.

measure → fix

Measured, not assumed

Nothing is defined before the first measurement. Properties and laws load when someone needs them — and once measured, they no longer change.

Only length and time. No kilograms. G = 1.
